# Do untreated fields form and renew a changed reader's copying neighborhood?

Freeze against 31d8e5b before examining new field outcomes. Run two consecutive blocks of 512 new seeds, 26689–27200 and 27201–27712. Use the stationary six-byte reference field with just the independent R..CLD founder, the same torus, first-free placement, retained memory, age limit 80, local fetch, external reads and 20,000 ppm copy substitutions as trait-lineage-01. Supply no partner or transport. Enroll readers born through tick 8192 inclusive. Give each reader the same 324-tick observation window from its birth. Simulate to tick 8516 plus 81 ticks for complete lifetime assays. Analyze every seed and record populations, stagnation, extinction and state hashes. Do not extend the seed budget in response to the outcome.

A changed reader has a different sequence from its immediate executor; both individuals must pass the established lifetime external-material and focal-only read-denial assay. This includes returns to an earlier sequence. For every changed reader record the presence of another living individual with identical bytes at a cardinal neighbor at birth and at any opening where the focal can execute. Record elapsed contact ticks, actual successful byte reads from such individuals, complete exact births from a single living match, and whether any such child also passes its own lifetime assay. A live match can be a passive template. Use within-case and whole-world denominators. Also screen all confirmed readers without the changed-executor restriction as a secondary comparison.

A starting copy requires a confirmed reader to execute a birth using all six input bytes from the same distinct living carrier of the reader's own sequence, in order and without substitution. The child must also be confirmed. To renew once, another such copy must be executed by two distinct later-born descendants (the new executor and the new living template), both confirmed. Every read in the new copy must occur strictly after both previous role holders' committed deaths. Both new role holders must descend from the preceding executor through confirmed same-sequence individuals, by actual division or intact aligned living donation. At least one of the two must be the preceding copy's child or descend from it along such a path. This tests a family supplying both roles, allows either descendant to execute, and excludes replacement by an unrelated matching resident. Apply the same transition recursively to count repeated renewals. Count the starting copy as depth zero and each qualifying replacement as one. Report worlds with at least one and at least two replacements, maximum depth and starter attrition. Individual identity includes birth, so reuse of an address never establishes continuity.

Validate the classifier on archived seed 20349 before prospective scoring. Its together branch must reproduce the known transfer by B from living A after the two original carriers died, with one replacement. Its ordinary and apart branches must give zero. Test recursion and rejection of early reads or unrelated donors separately. The controls are excluded from prospective rates. Archive all changed readers, their observed opportunities, stage counts, byte traces, permission results, and all renewal witnesses. Check the reference tests, and replay the fixed cohort with reverse traversal at a second optimization level. A negative result estimates incidence in these finite, untreated fields under this whole-lineage criterion.

# Focal forks after the geometry comparison

This witness study follows inspection of the geometry results. Include the union of (a) every focal reader whose window contained a compatible live east bank and (b) every reader mother whose field daughter had reader bytes. Follow each of those mothers and each actual reader daughter from its committed birth through age death, including the daughter born at the primary field cutoff. The 2,048-tick field classifications remain fixed.

Reconstruct each focal's actual world from the same seed and layout. Copy the complete world at focal birth. Run three counterfactuals from that state. Grant ordinary read access, deny only the focal CPU's external copy attempts sourcing its own current reservation, or deny all of its external copy attempts. Apply the intervention immediately before the copy and restore the ordinary mask immediately after that step. Keep other CPUs' permissions, all mutations, placement, reclamation and execution unchanged. Count all focal births, reader products, own-reservation reads and rejected attempts through natural death. Match ordinary branch products at or before the original cutoff to the field ledger. Compare exact input and output bytes to distinguish copy substitution from copying a preexisting reader sequence.

For each ordinary focal branch log opening east-bank type, occupant birth or reservation owner, visible bytes, count of opening-free cardinal neighbors, executed opcode and result through the first copy attempt or death. Ask whether an eastern live reader was accessible while the focal waited at reserve and what occupied the address when copying became possible. Report all selected cases, including forks that never transmit reader bytes.

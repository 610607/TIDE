# Natural inheritance from an independent six-byte ancestor

Baseline 20832ef. Fix the cohort and endpoints before running the fields. This is a detection pilot across 1,024 worlds. Use seeds 19521 through 20544 inclusive, one independently reproducing `R..CLD` at site 36, and no other initial occupants or code. This ancestor adds one inert instruction to the five-byte independent ancestor and contains no external source instruction. Retain the 16 × 4 torus, coordinate arbitration, first-free placement, retained bytes, age limit 80, external reading on, external fetch off, and 20,000 substitutions per million successful copies. Follow each natural world to tick 2048. Test independent founder reproduction separately in a mutation-free field with external reads on and off.

## Endpoints

Inspect every birth without filtering the mother's or daughter's genome. An external-material birth uses at least one byte read from a different site in the allocation that became that offspring. Enroll every CPU that produces such a birth by tick 2048. From each enrolled natural birth state, replay the focal's entire lifetime under ordinary permissions and separately under external-read denial for that CPU alone. Dependence requires ordinary offspring, at least one blocked external copy under denial and zero offspring under denial. Compare ordinary products within the horizon to the untreated birth record.

The primary outcome is a seed with a mother and her direct daughter whose sequences differ, where both meet the lifetime dependence criterion, the mother used external material to produce that daughter, and the daughter used external material to produce at least one grandchild by tick 2048. Count each changed mother–daughter edge once, irrespective of the number of grandchildren. Report unchanged edges separately. Secondary outcomes are a grandchild retaining the daughter's sequence, and complete intact transfers from an identified living external individual in both births. Identify the original input bytes, copy substitutions and bank types on candidate edges. Distinguish changes already present in the material read from changes introduced during copying.

Report every seed, census, terminal state hash, external-material births, confirmed dependent CPUs, changed edges, right-censored daughters still alive at the cutoff, and all candidate grandchild events. Retain stagnation, extinction and zero outcomes. Check replay in a separate optimized build with reverse traversal and compare against an independent untreated census. Stop the pilot at its fixed budget. A positive result calls for an independently prespecified cohort. A null result calls for identifying where transmission was lost before expanding the scan.

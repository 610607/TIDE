# Pilot 01 protocol

Declared before the evolutionary batch. This is a bounded search for a direct mother–daughter pair whose CPUs both used the enabled external channel and subsequently produced offspring. Include every seed and all births in the screen.

- Use one newborn independent ancestor `R.CLD` at (4,4) on an 8 by 8 torus. The neutral byte between reserve and copy allows a point substitution to introduce a read selector or jump while preserving the copy loop.
- Use seeds 1 through 8 inclusive, 512 ticks per seed, one slice per eligible CPU, next-tick newborns, coordinate priority, first-free placement, retained residual bytes, age limit 80 and substitution rate 20,000 per million successful writes. Run self-only, data-only and fetch-only for each seed. No warm-up or run replacement.
- A contact means a successful copy from an external bank or a successfully fetched external instruction, as appropriate. Count each birth after any such contact in the CPU's lifetime. A field candidate requires qualifying births at both ends of the same direct parent-to-daughter link. Record actual products even when the provider's sequence was copied. Report contacts with free and reserved banks too.
- For each candidate replay both field parents' newborn genomes separately. Use the first contacted bank's observed bytes and relative site as the donor background. In a bounded 5 by 5 arena put the focal CPU in the center with first-free placement, the donor as a live newborn at entry and all other sites empty. A non-live first contact is recorded as unresolved for this live-donor assay. Allow 64 ticks per generation, with mutation and age death off. For each successful first focal birth transfer that actual product to a fresh matched arena for up to three serial generations. Remove only the focal site's tested permission in the paired branch. Check the independent ancestor on the same background in both branches. Calibrate known read and fetch positive controls separately before running evolution.
- A confirmed pair requires both field parents to yield three serial births with use of the tested channel at each generation, with no such serial reproduction upon focal removal. Require an exact repeated dynamical state before the failed generation's first birth, or an explicit terminal failure. Require the independent ancestor to sustain three generations in both branches. Continued reproduction in both branches is permission-independent in this assay, with recorded timing available for rate comparisons. A timeout alone is inconclusive.
- At tick 512 classify an empty population as extinct, a living population with zero births in the final 80 ticks as stagnant, and any other living population as reproducing. Report per-arm seed incidence, births, deaths, population, contacts, candidate links, first candidate tick and final state hash. A null under this budget leaves the research question open.

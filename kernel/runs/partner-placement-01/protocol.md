# Does delivering a living matching template with a changed reader sustain its sequence?

Freeze the following comparison against revision 2daabd5 before running the branches. The unit is a naturally born daughter whose program differs from its executor and for which both individuals were already confirmed to require external reads. Use every such executor edge in the two six-origin blocks and the two new trait-lineage blocks. Exclude the previously replayed historical control from the latter study. A changed program can be a return to an older sequence. These selected cases measure a conditional intervention effect.

Recreate each stationary field from its original seed and verify the archived daughter birth hash. Keep the newborn in place. Supply an active, age-zero individual with the daughter's exact program and another with its executor's different program. In the together branch put the match at a cardinal neighbor of the daughter. In the apart branch exchange only the two supplied programs. Both branches share the occupied footprint, ages, default newborn CPUs, background residents and clock. Record an unmodified branch for every case. There is one delivery, followed by ordinary dynamics.

Protect the daughter and the executor's site. Accept free sites or occupied sites without reservations. Select the near site in east, south, west, north order (the newborn faces east). Select the distant site at toroidal Manhattan distance at least four, preferring free, then greatest distance, then lowest index. If a resident needs moving, exchange it with the lowest-index remaining free site outside the protected and delivery sites. Process near first. Preserve its age and rebase its local pointers with the existing transport rule. Use the same relocations for both treatment arms. If either site or parking space is unavailable, retain the exclusion and its ordinary outcome. Retained free bytes at a delivery site may be overwritten in both arms.

Each confirmed individual must have an external-material offspring, match its ordinary lifetime birth replay, encounter blocked external copying under its own read denial, and have zero births under denial. Supplied individuals are never roots. Record exact direct confirmed descendants, exact focal-executed copies from the supplied living match, and primary witnesses descended through such a copy. Record per-branch population outcomes, including extinction and stagnation.

Compare together and apart within the same eligible case. Report discordant cases and seed-level paired counts (a seed succeeds if any of its eligible cases succeeds), separated by archive block. Report ordinary controls and all exclusions. The ordinary focal lifetime must reproduce the historical assay. Require identical tables with reverse traversal and a second optimization level. These manipulations test the sufficiency of one delivery in observed fields; spontaneous neighborhood transmission is a separate question.

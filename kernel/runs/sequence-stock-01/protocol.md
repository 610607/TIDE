# Whole-field sequence abundance and copy provenance

Baseline 95b77dc. Freeze this observational census before computing cohort results. Replay both complete, untreated six-origin-01 cohorts (seeds 19521–20544 and 20545–21568), from their single independent six-byte ancestor through tick 2048. Include all outcomes. Seed 19722 and sequence `R..PLD` are a previously known witness selected for a time-series illustration. Keep the cohorts separate and report the pooled counts.

At every committed tick including zero, count exact six-byte strings in occupied, reserved and free banks separately. Include arbitrary bytes, including zero, and exclude never-written empty banks. Total stored matches are the sum of those three mutually exclusive counts. An occupied count is a count of individuals. A free or reserved match describes readable storage and may be a partial assembly. Record change points so that each time series, including zeros after disappearance, is recoverable. Measure the longest uninterrupted run of positive occupied abundance and of positive stored abundance separately, in observed tick snapshots. Mark presence at the cutoff.

For every completed birth, retain executor identity and sequence, destination identity, product, and all six source sites, identities or reservation owners, source offsets, read times, opening bank contents and input bytes. Assign each birth one route. A copy substitution takes precedence whenever any input differs from its written byte. Otherwise a single intact template requires aligned offsets and the entire source bank equal to the final product at every read. Distinguish the executor's own sequence, one other continuously identified living individual, and one external site in free or reserved states throughout the reads. Classify other unchanged inputs as assembly. Separately record whether the executor's sequence equals the product and whether any external bytes were used.

The main result is the number of births, worlds and distinct sequences for intact live-template copying by an executor whose own sequence differs from the product. A secondary result counts births restoring a previously occupied sequence that had zero occupied copies at the opening of that tick. Report their routes. For intact self-copy and intact living-neighbor copy separately, trace material ancestry through the actual supplier. Report the longest same-route path in transfers and the longest first-carrier-birth to last-product-birth span in ticks. These paths describe realized transmission, not causal life extension. Residual banks have no CPU birth identity.

Archive cohort and sequence summaries, indexed checksums of all detailed world ledgers and the complete known witness trajectory. Provide a single-seed replay entry point.

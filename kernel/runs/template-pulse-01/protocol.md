# One matching template in a natural reader's first allocation

Freeze before observing any treated branch. Baseline 57345eb. Enroll all 40 changed daughters from the primary six-origin-01 endpoint, 31 from the pilot and nine from replication. Keep every daughter in the denominator, grouping shared worlds by seed. Use each archived birth-state hash, original field configuration, copy substitutions and deterministic random context.

Fork each daughter at birth into ordinary and matched-template arms. Start the intervention at the first allocation won by that daughter. Pin one virtual template to the first external address it attempts to copy from while holding that allocation. The template is the daughter's six birth bytes in their original order. Only the enrolled CPU reading that one address can see the substituted input buffer. Use its real read offsets and the normal bounds, permissions, write pointer, destination and copy-mutation operator. The buffer has length six even if the underlying bank is shorter. Self-reads and reads from other addresses use their ordinary bytes. The original bank keeps its contents, CPU, ownership and visibility to all other individuals. End the pulse at division (successful or failed) or focal death, whichever comes first. Later allocations receive ordinary inputs. This is a digital intervention on template access. It supplies no additional executing individual.

The primary case-level endpoint requires a grandchild born from the first allocation with all six daughter bytes, that grandchild's ordinary reproduction and zero lifetime offspring with focal external reads denied (including at least one blocked external copy), and at least one ordinary great-grandchild carrying the same six bytes. Follow enrolled daughters and the first-allocation grandchild to age death even beyond the natural enrollment cutoff. Fork each such grandchild from its actual birth state with the pulse removed. Follow altered grandchildren as well. Report failed allocations and nonreproducers, exact products at both generations, ordinary and denied births, and the subset of exact great-grandchildren incorporating external bytes. Record input/output bytes and source addresses, actual bank contents and template use separately. Count one success per daughter irrespective of product count.

Validate ordinary daughter birth products against all 57 archived grandchildren, verify natural birth hashes and compare both arms through pulse onset. Report population outcomes and final hashes. Replay with forward and reverse cell traversal in separate optimized builds. The macromolecule analogy describes the program sequence, template and product as distinct roles in a discrete copying event. The simulator measures execution steps and byte transfers. No molecular reactions or kinetic parameters are assigned.

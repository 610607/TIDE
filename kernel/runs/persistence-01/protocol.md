# Persistence of the prospective fetch families

Declared after inspecting the 512-tick horizon study and before extending field time. Include all eight distinct confirmed maternal families from the seven affected prospective fetch seeds. Follow each of those original seeds through tick 4,096 with every physical setting, seed and ancestor from the 512-tick run. Report checkpoints at 512, 1,024, 2,048 and 4,096. Require exact agreement with the archived field hash and family ledger at 512 before accepting the continuation.

A clonal member has an unbroken parentage path with identical genomic bytes from the recorded family root. Record births, last birth, depth, living members and exact extinction tick. Once the last member dies that family remains extinct. Count births of the same bytes outside the focal family separately. Split those births into inheritance from an already identical parent and births to a different-genome parent. Report every selected field world and family, including extinct families, and whether the surrounding field population continues reproducing. Selection here is conditional on prior confirmation, so these seed counts measure persistence of known families only.

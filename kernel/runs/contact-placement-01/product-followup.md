Supplement to the frozen placement protocol. Freeze this observation rule before scoring their later reuse. Use the existing intervened fields, preserving the one earlier swap. Follow each offspring from the first instruction opening after birth until age death or tick 2048. Allow a first aligned write in this window to finish by the earlier of death plus 81 and tick 2048. Score the same six unchanged, aligned reads from this particular living offspring followed by division. Retained bytes and self-copy by the focal offspring do not qualify. Report all denominators and right truncation. A second traced link establishes one further transfer. Sustained replication over indefinite generations is outside the observation window.

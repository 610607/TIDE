# Daughter placement and a prospective natural transmission screen

Fixed before executing either experiment. Use VM v1, repository baseline bd23917. A reader is the five-byte program RSCLD. The reference field is the 16 × 4 torus with one independent site-36 ancestor, age limit 80, first-free placement, retained memory, external reads enabled, fetch disabled and 20,000 substitutions per million successful copies.

## Matched placement

Use all 64 archived background seeds 577–640 without selecting for reader outcomes. Grow each reference field and inspect committed ticks 64 through 256 inclusive. Choose the latest tick with a free center and all four cardinal neighbors free and at least one occupied site at toroidal Manhattan distance two from the center. At that tick prefer the most occupied distance-two ring, then the lowest flat center site. Count an absent starting state as a setup failure. No sample replacement.

In three copies of that exact background introduce newborn RSCLD CPUs at the center and immediately east. Add exactly two occupied banks. All other state is as in the background. For the mother's first reserve attempt make west, north or south its first choice by setting its initial bearing for that instruction only. Restore its ordinary eastern bearing immediately after the step. Competition, mutation and every later instruction, including all daughter instructions, use the ordinary rules. The eastern bank is occupied by the donor and cannot be a daughter placement. The normal initial bearing already tries north first. Archive the common background and restored states, and include lost contests and altered first products in the denominator.

Register the target, first allocation, mother and daughter identity before observing the daughter's offspring. A first link requires that the mother won the designated opening claim and copied all five unchanged bytes from the original living donor into an exact reader daughter. If the first link fails, score the assigned arm zero. Follow each qualifying daughter over its entire lifetime, recording every birth, each successful copy's opening source kind/site/birth or owner, source offsets, input and output, maternal survival at each copy, and blocked attempts. Fork the daughter's birth with ordinary permissions and focal external reads denied. Also deny the mother's external reads from introduction in a paired run. Remove each mask at focal death. Report cohort, setup, contest, first-link and lifetime denominators; record world births/deaths, end counts, hashes and final-80 status at 256 steps after introduction for all branches.

The primary matched endpoint is at least one exact grandchild copied entirely and unchanged from any one living external RSCLD by the qualifying daughter, with no focal births under mother denial and under daughter denial. The original mother as donor is a secondary attribution. Compare west against north and south separately, report case-level discordances by seed. Conditions have different target neighborhoods, mutation contexts and competitors. This measures the complete placement intervention. No daughter read pointer is steered.

## Independent untreated fields

Run 8,192 disjoint seeds 3137–11328 inclusive for 2,048 ticks per seed. No introduced readers and no steering. Analyze all births with the lineage study's all-birth screen. The primary outcome is a direct mother–daughter–grandchild chain of two exact live-reader transfers. Each link must use five unchanged bytes from a single identified living external reader. Independently remove only the mother's or daughter's external reads in lifetime forks of their respective natural birth states. Both must reproduce in the ordinary branch and have zero births under denial. Report raw chains, confirmed chains, distinct positive seeds and right censoring. Retain all seeds, late allocations, population stagnation and extinction. Also report all one-link transfers, placement relative to the mother, daughter outcomes, and the separate established 4,096-tick paired behavioral assay, which admits changed genomes. Direct permission forks include every reader mother and daughter of a natural intact link, irrespective of the second-link outcome.

Keep controlled branches and natural seed incidence separate. Follow-up permission forks can run beyond 2,048 to score a focal lifetime; only births by tick 2,048 count for natural incidence. Replay with reversed field traversal and a separately optimized build. Stop at the fixed budgets, including if zero chains are found.

# One public vacancy during a live donor encounter

Frozen before inspecting intervention outcomes or new seeds. Test whether space for a daughter is a limiting condition when an east-looking reader already has an intact live donor. The VM is v1 at 1b4debb. Use the original 16 × 4 torus, site-36 independent ancestor, read-only external access, age limit 80, first-free placement, retained memory and 20,000 substitutions per million successful copies.

## Case selection

1. Reconstruct the three previously observed eastern encounters in seeds 419, 561 and 569, [geometry study](../geometry-01/README.md). A baseline-only inspection found two, three and three eligible non-eastern neighbors respectively at the focal birth states.
2. Independently run exactly seeds 577–1088 inclusive for 2,048 ticks each. Census every newborn exact RSCLD, all first copy attempts and every first eligible encounter. An encounter is the first opening at which the focal has yet to attempt C, is eligible to execute its local R without a reservation and sees another occupied RSCLD immediately east. Record cases even if a neighbor is free, competing or due to die. Observe openings for instructions 1 through 2,048. Record every seed, all births, deaths, final population and births during the final 80 ticks.
3. A blocked case has zero opening-free neighbors. At that opening an eligible vacancy target is an occupied northern, southern or western neighbor whose CPU holds no allocation. Prefer north, then south, then west, independent of outcomes. Record all ineligible cases. Use the preferred local target for primary comparisons. Also test the other eligible directions in the three historical cases as sensitivity checks. Make the intervention once. The ordinary arbitration still selects the winner.

## Exact field forks

Fork the complete committed opening state. Compare ordinary continuation, release of the selected local bank, and release of one distant bank. A release immediately retires one allocation-free CPU, makes its site free and preserves all visible bytes. Leave all other sites and site permissions intact. The removal subtracts one from the opening live count. Validate the resource ledger and the exact state outside that site. An out-of-tree build adapter exposes this intervention only for these counterfactuals. The reference kernel and its dynamics stay frozen.

The distant target must also be an allocation-free CPU at toroidal Manhattan distance at least three from the focal and at least two from its eastern donor. Choose deterministically by lowest byte mismatch count to the local target (including length difference), then smallest absolute difference in birth tick, then lowest flat site. Report match quality. For each local fork also deny external reads only to the focal founder during its lifetime as a permission control. All other permissions and all mutation draws use the ordinary rules. For free openings and blocked cases lacking an eligible local target report ordinary continuation only. If no distant match exists, keep the remaining branches and flag the missing control.

## Endpoints

- Primary endpoint for each branch is a first focal birth carrying RSCLD whose five unmutated copies all came from the exact eastern individual alive at the fork, identified by its site and birth tick. Also record time to winning allocation, first copy, first birth, and opening occupancy and copy provenance. A different offspring or no birth counts as zero.
- Follow actual descendants through 512 ticks after the fork. Founder depth is zero. Count only uninterrupted parent-to-daughter transmissions in which a reader parent copied all five RSCLD input bytes externally, without substitution, into a reader daughter. Track the maximum depth, lineage births, living members, and birth cohorts. Distinguish original donor, other live donors, own-reservation reads, other reservations and free residual bytes. Report when such a chain uses a live donor for every link.
- Report historical and prospective results separately. Report primary local-versus-ordinary and local-versus-distant discordances per eligible individual and distinct seed incidence. Sister branches and alternate directions are repeated measures. Report intervened read transmission separately from the incidence of spontaneous inheritance. The prospective seed budget and 512-tick follow-up do not depend on the results.

# Behavioral transmission and spatial opportunity

Declared before examining changed offspring or reader neighborhoods. Use the v1 field and the 4,096-tick mutation-free paired assay from the [horizon study](../horizon-01/README.md). The observer may inspect opening snapshots. Only the original VM advances them.

## Fetch pedigree

Replay the eight confirmed prospective maternal roots in their seven original fetch fields through tick 4,096. Include every genealogical descendant, whether it divided, contacted a donor, kept the genome or changed it. Identify every parent–daughter birth with different genomic bytes within each rooted pedigree, including changes from later descendants. Count exact identities by site and birth tick. Reconcile all four field checkpoints and the original clonal family at tick 4,096 with the persistence records.

For each changed link compare the two newborn genomes on the same donor bytes and direction as the earliest confirmed maternal event of that root (ordered by field tick, then daughter site). A retained capacity requires both genotypes to satisfy the frozen three-generation dependence criterion in that shared assay, with the ancestor viable in both branches. Record the actual copy substitutions separately from any genomic change caused by the bytes copied. Also assay each field individual's own first donor if it was occupied, and report unoccupied, reserved and never-contacted cases. Field-supported transmission additionally requires the parent to have contacted its own donor by the changed birth and the daughter to divide after its contact, with both own-donor assays dependent. Report changed births, distinct roots, cumulative descendants, last birth, last death and living descendants at the endpoint. An independent origin elsewhere is outside this pedigree.

## Read opportunity

Replay read-only seeds 1–64 for 512 ticks. Before seeing these counts, reserve seeds 65–320 as a separate prospective cohort of 256 read-only runs at the same duration. Include every seed and every newborn with the previously observed reader genome `RSCLD`, including newborns with no copy attempt. The reader selects its eastern bank. Survey the committed birth state and every opening state through the first local copy attempt inclusive, death, or the endpoint. Record the opening bank at that first attempt and all successful reads and births through the endpoint. Record the closing state at 512 for a newborn alive there which has not attempted a copy. Count a reader once per exposure category.

Define a compatible live donor by testing each distinct occupied neighbor sequence as an eastern newborn donor in the frozen read assay. Compatibility requires a focal read in each of three transferred generations and the ordinary denial and ancestor controls. For each reader report functional compatibility at birth, anywhere in its four-site neighborhood before or at the first attempt, in the addressed east position over that window, and at the attempt itself. Occupancy, free residual bytes and reserved bytes are distinct. Independently count exact `RSCLD` byte matches in all bank types, and whether another live exact reader existed anywhere on the lattice. This all-field exact count excludes the focal site. An off-axis donor indicates a spatial opportunity only under a different orientation.

Report every reader birth, its actual product sequences and the fate of the product's read behavior. Apply the original two-generation field screen across all read genotypes, with the 4,096-tick paired test for occupied first donors. Keep counts of candidates with non-live first donors. Report the historical and prospective cohorts separately. Verify the historical seed hashes and exposed births. This study ends at its declared seed range and tick budgets.

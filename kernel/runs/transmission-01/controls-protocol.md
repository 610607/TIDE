Put a newborn focal reader at (4,4), an independent ancestor at (2,2), and three live newborn donor banks at (5,4), (5,5) and (5,6). Observe all 512 ticks in each arrangement. Compare normal access against removal only at the focal founder during its lifetime, restoring the site permission after death. Also run both permission conditions with an independent `R.CLD` focal founder for each layout. Seal the conditions before running. Transfer no individuals and replace no donors. With mutation disabled, each setup has one deterministic trajectory. Record all focal-descended births with CPU parent, actual copy sources and product, and every final population status.

Report the number and maximum parent-to-child depth of continuous reader lineages descended from the founder. At each transmission require a reader parent, a reader daughter and a successful external read in copying that daughter. Founder depth is zero. Also count daughters that themselves transmitted reader bytes. All sibling births count separately for birth totals and once per depth for lineage length.

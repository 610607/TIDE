# Horizon sensitivity and prospective validation

Frozen before executing any extended assay or generating field seeds 33–64. Compare 64 and 4,096 ticks per generation. The longer limit is a fixed 64-fold increase in the observation budget. A repeated state or first focal birth stops that generation early. Freeze all v1 field, arena, permission, mutation and classification rules from the [discovery](../pilot-01/protocol.md) and [replication](../replication-01/protocol.md) protocols.

1. Reassay all candidate births from seeds 1–8 and 9–32 whose two first contacts were occupied banks. Keep the original rows and classifications. Count all excluded events and their seeds. Report the 16 previously identified replication events with three allowed generations and a denied timeout at both ends separately.
2. Run seeds 33–64 inclusive, 512 ticks in each of the three original arms. Use the original field runner and its unchanged 64-tick assay. Measure every eligible candidate at both horizons. This third cohort tests the longer assay prospectively. Report the three cohorts separately. Count seeds with a confirmed pair out of all seeds in each arm. Multiple births from one family count once at seed level.
3. For each eligible pair reconstruct both field genomes and live first-donor backgrounds. Test both focal permissions and both independent ancestor controls. Transfer the actual first daughter through three fresh arenas. Report per-generation elapsed ticks, byte source use, product bytes, and the first two equal complete dynamical states when a cycle occurs. Use generation-relative ticks with the initialized arena at tick zero. Full byte equality decides a cycle. The time and birth counters can be omitted because age death and mutation are off. A timeout remains unresolved. At 64 ticks every replay must reproduce all archived scores and classifications.
4. For each confirmed seed, audit the parent–daughter–granddaughter links against the field event stream, verify that the qualifying contact occurred by each birth and record the origin, last birth, depth and final living count of each mother's contiguous same-genome family. Report how many transmitting links retained identical bytes through all three field individuals. Keep exact eligibility, event, seed and family denominators.
5. Replay archived fixed fetch-dependent and read-dependent controls and the independent ancestor. Verify the seed 8 discovery case. Stop at the declared seed range and horizon. A finding in this assay concerns access to a neighbor's bytes in the specified arena. The assay does not manipulate the donor's own CPU.

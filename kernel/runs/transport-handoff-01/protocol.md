# Does passive exchange increase handoff to a different executor?

Freeze before scoring mobile-field handoffs or running new seeds. Use repository cde5beb and the ordinary spatial-transport-01 settings. Each field starts with the supplied R..CLD at site 36 on the 16 × 4 torus. All newborns face east. The only variable is the existing post-commit mobile-bank exchange rule, with thresholds 0, 1 or 16 out of 64 per selected matching edge (east, low and high). Keep retained bytes, local fetch, neighbor read, first-free placement, maximum age 80, 20,000 ppm substitution and a 2,048-tick horizon. There is no targeted placement.

First reconstruct seeds 19521–21568 in all three arms. Require identical population and encounter ledgers, hashes and enrolled births to the archived transport experiment and identical handoff links to the stationary east arm of the bearing study. For a prospective comparison, use all 2,048 consecutive seeds 21569–23616 in each arm. Retain every field, including extinction, stagnation and zero enrollments. Report historical and prospective results separately and combine them descriptively. Within each cohort the three arms share seeds.

Enroll every six-byte offspring made from six aligned, unchanged reads of one living neighbor by an executor with a different genome. Main analyses require the offspring to be observed until age death (born by tick 1967). Search for its subsequent use as a living donor by any other individual, including executors with the same sequence. Track individuals by birth site and tick through displacement. Classify the executor of each onward birth as the original maker or a different individual. Score a post-death handoff only if its first read strictly follows the maker's death. Archive all onward links, including links from incompletely followed newborns. Predeclare a sensitivity analysis restricted to offspring born by tick 1886, which gives any opening up to donor death another full lifespan for division to finish. Record truncated enrollments separately.

Primary outcome is worlds with one or more different-executor handoffs per 1,000 unselected worlds. Compare high and low separately with stationary east. Report counts of discordant seed pairs and exact two-sided paired binary tests at equal discordance probabilities. Report paired rate differences and approximate 95% intervals using original seed as unit. These two contrasts are descriptive with no multiple-testing adjustment. Secondary measures include handed-off newborns per 1,000 fully observed enrolled newborns, the number handed off after maker death, original-maker reuse, and the number of qualifying first-generation births per field. Distinguish exchange-driven changes in exposure from the conditional frequency among successfully created offspring. Verify complete field and product tables with reversed traversal and a second optimization level. These bounded field trials measure natural encounters under the specified lattice exchange, without adding a kernel rule.

# Can a copying relationship survive turnover?

**One copying relationship renewed after a partner was supplied; none renewed in untreated fields.** Heritable dependence evolved separately for neighbor reads and instruction fetch ([stage report](neighbor-dependence-results.md) · [summary](first-stage-summary.md)); neither establishes relationship persistence. Identical bytes [recurred outside tracked families](../../kernel/runs/persistence-01/families.tsv). Read-dependent lineages crossed a program change and reproduced after root death in [4/1,024 worlds](../../kernel/runs/trait-lineage-01/results/summary.tsv), but those later births carried the original sequence. Even [five consecutive live-reader transfers](../../kernel/runs/inheritance-01/README.md) did not establish repeated replacement of both roles.

## Untreated fields: few starts, no confirmed renewal

[Natural-renewal-01](../../kernel/runs/natural-renewal-01/protocol.md) used 1,024 new stationary 16 × 4 worlds with one independent six-byte founder, neighbor reads, local fetch and retained bytes. Enrollment ended at tick 8,192; follow-up was 324 ticks from birth. In 30 worlds, 35 confirmed readers differed from their confirmed executors. None had an adjacent exact living match at birth or during execution; the supplementary census found 14 with a match elsewhere and 21 with none anywhere. Among 11,750 confirmed readers, eight worlds yielded an intact copy from a living match; seven yielded a confirmed child from that copy. [Summary](../../kernel/runs/natural-renewal-01/results/summary.tsv) · [Contacts](../../kernel/runs/natural-renewal-01/results/contacts.tsv) · [Starting births](../../kernel/runs/natural-renewal-01/results/starts.tsv)

A renewal required a confirmed birth from six aligned, unsubstituted bytes, all read from one living template strictly after both previous participants died. Both replacements (executor and template) had to be distinct, later-born descendants of the preceding executor along confirmed same-sequence paths through division or intact living donation. At least one had to be the preceding copy's child or descend from it. An initial template could be passive; unconfirmed successors and unrelated handoffs did not count. **0/1,024 worlds** reached either one renewal or at least two in succession (exact one-sided 95% upper bound 0.292% per independent world, under this definition and window).

The [seven-family replay](../../kernel/runs/natural-renewal-01/starter-audit/README.md) found three overlapping pairs of eligible descendants after both deaths, two adjacent pairs and one complete write. In seed 27266 the executor died of age with division pending. The [family counts](../../kernel/runs/natural-renewal-01/starter-audit/results/families.tsv) and [tick traces](../../kernel/runs/natural-renewal-01/starter-audit/results/ticks.tsv) show losses before pairing and after copying, not which bottleneck dominates.

## Supplied partner: one replacement is possible

[Partner-placement-01](../../kernel/runs/partner-placement-01/README.md) delivered two individuals in 46 of 75 archived changed-reader cases (35 eligible seeds), swapping matching and nonmatching programs between near and far sites at fixed initial occupancy and relocations. The endpoint was a confirmed same-sequence descendant born after focal death, allowing intervening changes, not replacement of both roles: 3/46 near, 1/46 far (one shared, two near-only), 0/75 unmodified. The unmodified branch lacked the extra occupants and relocations. [Paired counts](../../kernel/runs/partner-placement-01/results/paired.tsv) · [Branch ledger](../../kernel/runs/partner-placement-01/results/branches.tsv)

In seed **20349**, the original executor (12) and supplied template (13) both died at tick 1308. Descendant 83 executed the copy using living descendant 76 for all six bytes at ticks 1315–1325, producing confirmed child 121 at tick 1327. The [classifier replay](../../kernel/runs/natural-renewal-01/controls.py) of the [byte and lifetime records](../../kernel/runs/partner-placement-01/results/nodes.tsv.gz) scored depth one near, zero far and unmodified. The pedigree ended without a second renewal. The synthetic depth-two fixture tests scoring only; neither control enters the untreated rate. Living-source use does not establish dependence on the source CPU's execution.

## Population studies: permissions, reclamation and type persistence

- **Access.** [Access-dynamics-01](../../kernel/runs/access-dynamics-01/README.md) ran 32 matched seeds per arm on 32 × 32 for 4,194,304 ticks. Reads plus fetch versus self-only increased Bedau's mean new activity (P4; median paired difference +1,833, Holm p = 3.0 × 10⁻⁴), beyond the neutral shadow. No MODES endpoint differed; reads versus self and adding fetch to reads were inconclusive. P4 here measures accumulated genotype presence, not adaptation or renewal. [Endpoint tests](../../kernel/runs/access-dynamics-01/results/analysis/tests.tsv)
- **Reclamation.** [Dead-code-01](../../kernel/runs/dead-code-01/README.md) used 32 new matched seeds per cell at the same size and horizon. It replicated the full-access versus self-only P4 result under retention (+1,478; Holm p = 1.1 × 10⁻⁴). Retain versus empty, within either the read or read_fetch arm, changed no primary endpoint; both claims were inconclusive, not equivalent overall. The only differing mechanism endpoint (M2) went against the raw-material prediction; [post hoc counts](../../kernel/runs/dead-code-01/results/posthoc/zero-births.tsv) traced most of the excess under clearing to all-zero offspring. Filtered `RCCCJD` counts of 21/32 retain versus 9/32 empty in read_fetch were descriptive. [Claim tests](../../kernel/runs/dead-code-01/results/analysis/claims.tsv) · [Replication](../../kernel/runs/dead-code-01/results/analysis/replication.tsv)
- **Size.** [Lattice-size-01](../../kernel/runs/lattice-size-01/README.md) held access (read_fetch), reclamation (retain), seeds and measurement settings fixed across 24, 32, 48 and 64 square lattices. Fetch-requiring `RCCCJD` passed the fixed 1,024-generation filter in 4, 12, 28 and 31 of 32 worlds, respectively. All five primary endpoints differed, including filter-free presence. The rule attributed this to size, not filter length, without separating site count from side length. [Size tests](../../kernel/runs/lattice-size-01/results/analysis/tests.tsv) · [Attribution](../../kernel/runs/lattice-size-01/results/analysis/attribution.tsv). Type presence, descendant survival and [fetch-source shares](../../kernel/runs/lattice-size-01/results/analysis/secondary.tsv) do not trace both roles across turnover. Borrowed fetch is not living-template copying.

**Next decision:** Establish whether starters can replace both roles repeatedly before testing a population effect at fixed access. Failure after enough well-followed starts would constrain renewal; another zero with few starts would constrain world-level incidence but leave renewal conditional on starting unresolved. Neither is evidence of no population effect. The observed losses do not yet support choosing a placement or timing intervention.

The [untreated renewal feasibility note](natural-renewal-feasibility.md) estimated the recruitment needed to constrain renewal after a start. The [completed fixed follow-up](../../kernel/runs/natural-renewal-02/findings.md) found 31 starts in 29 of 5,300 fresh worlds and no renewals, giving a one-sided exact 95% upper bound of 9.81% conditional on a starter-bearing world. A post hoc [eligibility audit](../../kernel/runs/natural-renewal-02/eligibility-audit/README.md) found that most lineage exits copied another program without error, not substitution or early death. A draft, not agreed, [fetch-renewal question](fetch-renewal-rq.md) proposes tracing provider identity in the lattice-size-01 worlds, where `RCCCJD` persists at 64 × 64.

# Supplementary order contrast

The primary survival tables had been produced and the final exact-family counts and median loss times inspected before specifying this supplement. Freeze the comparison before running any reordered founder or scoring its outcome.

At each archived first birth, fork the fed breakage chamber again, rotating that one completed sequence left by one position. Preserve its composition, the rest of the chamber and all random streams. Report whole-world accounts for every branch. Use roots for which the rotation changes the unordered pair of the founder sequence and its bitwise complement to measure order-specific effects. Report excluded roots. For each branch and nonoverlapping encounter windows 1–128, 129–256, 257–512, 513–1024, 1025–1536 and 1537–2048, compute the fraction of newly completed products in each of the two competing sequence pairs. The symmetric paired effect is half the original-arm preference for the original pair plus half the rotation-arm preference for the rotated pair. Assign zero preference to empty birth windows. Average across matched worlds, with a paired normal-approximation 95% interval, and report both blocks. Score all births irrespective of ancestry. This detects a population-level compositional signal even when physical templates turn over or an exact pedigree breaks.
